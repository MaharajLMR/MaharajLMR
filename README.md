### Hi, I'm Maharaj

I'm a third-year mechatronics student at Chennai Institute of Technology. Most of my time goes into ROS 2, simulation, and the maths behind robot arms and mobile robots. In November I start a research internship on parallel robots at IIT Madras.

Things I've built:

**[resilient-agv-fleet](https://github.com/MaharajLMR/resilient-agv-fleet)**: five warehouse AGVs in Gazebo sharing aisles that only fit one robot. I wrote the traffic manager that keeps them from crashing into each other or getting stuck (C++, plus a Python version I use to try ideas out). It also handles robots breaking down, batteries running low and the warehouse system going offline. It started as my entry for the Bonfiglioli Smart Motion Hackathon, where it placed 2nd runner-up.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MaharajLMR/resilient-agv-fleet/main/docs/fleet_showcase.gif" width="560">

**[cnc-lathe-arm](https://github.com/MaharajLMR/cnc-lathe-arm)**: a ROS 2 and Gazebo rebuild of the jib arm my team made for the DriveNext RANE hackathon (1st runner-up). It has pot knob control through an ADS1115, a web HMI, and picks a part and puts it in a lathe chuck in simulation. It is a rebuild, not the hackathon code.
